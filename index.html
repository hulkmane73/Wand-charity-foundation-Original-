About Our Foundation

Learn about our mission, vision, and the impact we're making in communities.

🌍

Compassion in Action Since 2018

Wand Charity Foundation was born out of a deep desire to serve the most vulnerable populations in Uganda. We believe that every person deserves access to basic necessities — food, clean water, education, and healthcare.

Our team works tirelessly on the ground, partnering with local leaders and volunteers to create sustainable change. From rural villages to urban slums, we go where the need is greatest.
